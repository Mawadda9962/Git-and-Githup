The map was not meant to be found—at least, that’s what Elin believed the moment her fingers brushed the brittle parchment hidden beneath the loose floorboard of her family’s cottage. Inked in fading silver lines that shimmered as though alive, it marked no village she knew, no river that followed any honest path. Yet, when she unfolded it, a soft whisper curled through the air, calling her name like a secret long buried. In that instant, the quiet rhythms of her small life cracked open, and the forest beyond the fields—the one no one dared enter—seemed to be waiting.
