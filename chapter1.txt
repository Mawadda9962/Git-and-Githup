The map was not meant to be found—at least, that’s what Elin believed the moment her fingers brushed the brittle parchment hidden beneath the loose floorboard of her family’s cottage. Inked in fading silver lines that shimmered as though alive, it marked no village she knew, no river that followed any honest path. Yet, when she unfolded it, a soft whisper curled through the air, calling her name like a secret long buried. In that instant, the quiet rhythms of her small life cracked open, and the forest beyond the fields—the one no one dared enter—seemed to be waiting.


Elara held the map up to the fading light, watching as its pale markings shimmered like moonlight on water. The lines did not stay still; they shifted and curled, revealing hidden paths that vanished when she blinked. In one corner, a symbol flared brighter than the rest—a small, twisting emblem that seemed to pulse in time with her heartbeat. When she leaned closer, she could almost hear it, a faint whisper threading through her thoughts, guiding her eyes toward a path that led straight into the heart of the forbidden woods.

She hesitated only a moment before resolve settled over her like a second skin. Whatever magic lived within the map had chosen her, and she could not ignore its call. Grabbing her satchel, she tucked the parchment safely inside and slipped out into the cool evening air. The village behind her glowed warm and familiar, but she did not look back. Ahead, the Whispering Woods stirred, their shadows deep and watchful, and with a steady breath, Elara stepped forward to meet whatever awaited her within.


The moment Elara crossed into the Whispering Woods, the air seemed to change, growing cooler and thick with a hush that swallowed even the sound of her footsteps. Towering trees arched overhead, their twisted branches weaving a dim green canopy that filtered the light into shifting patterns. A faint murmur drifted between the trunks—too soft to understand, yet impossible to ignore—raising goosebumps along her arms. Fear stirred within her, sharp and unfamiliar, but beneath it pulsed a deeper thrill, a quiet certainty that she was exactly where she was meant to be, standing at the edge of something vast and unknowable.
