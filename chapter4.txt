The air grew cold and heavy, as if the very stones were breathing in slow, forgotten rhythms. Elara stepped carefully over shattered stone and tangled roots that wound through the floor like veins of the past. Faint symbols glowed along the cracked walls, pulsing weakly in response to her presence, as though the tower itself recognized her arrival. Somewhere above, a distant creak echoed through the hollow spiral stairs, and with each step she took upward, the sense of ancient magic grew sharper—watchful, waiting, and dangerously alive.
