Elara, a bright-eyed villager with wind-tangled hair and restless curiosity, stood clutching the fragile map in trembling hands. Though fear fluttered in her chest, her courage burned brighter. The strange markings called to her, promising secrets beyond the quiet fields she had always longed to leave behind.


Shadow is a sleek, silver-eyed wolf with fur as dark as midnight, moving like a whisper through the trees. Fiercely loyal to Elara, he watches over her with quiet intelligence and unshakable devotion. Though wild at heart, he senses her courage and chooses to stand beside her, guardian and guide.
