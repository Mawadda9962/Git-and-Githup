
When Elara returned to her village, she was no longer just a curious girl with a strange map—she was a bearer of renewal. Crops grew richer, rivers ran clearer, and travelers began to arrive, drawn by the revived forest’s gentle magic. The Whispering Woods no longer warned people away; instead, they welcomed them in. And at its edge, Elara often stood with Shadow by her side, smiling as she listened to the woods whisper not of secrets, but of gratitude.
